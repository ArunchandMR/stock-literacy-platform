Mentor's Framework

Why no hard stop-loss?

Cash equity only — no leverage, no F&O. Fundamentally strong businesses. A 5% global shock is temporary noise; the business value doesn't change overnight.

The positional safety net

If a swing trade hits volatility, it converts to a short-term positional hold (1–3 weeks). Strong fundamentals + historical support absorb the drawdown naturally.
