📖 The 4 Stop-Loss Methods Explained

  • Fixed 2% — Legacy reference. Often too tight for Indian equities.
  • 1.5× ATR — Volatility-adjusted. Recommended primary method.
  • 20-EMA Line — Structural trend anchor. Dynamic, moves with price.
  • Swing Low — Absolute floor of the 21-session consolidation box.

Compare the Stop Method columns above. The ATR stop empirically captures more winners by giving volatile Indian equities room to breathe before moving up.

📖 MAE & MFE — What They Mean

  • MAE (Max Adverse Excursion) — How far a trade went against you before recovering. Use to calibrate your stop.
  • MFE (Max Favorable Excursion) — The peak profit a trade reached. Use to calibrate your GTT target.
  • Profit Factor ≥ 1.75 — Target threshold. PF = gross wins ÷ gross losses.
  • Expectancy > 0 — Expected return per unit risked. This is the real edge metric.

A 70% win-rate strategy can lose money (70×1% − 30×4% = −50). A 45% strategy with good R:R can make money. Expectancy and Profit Factor are the only metrics that matter.
